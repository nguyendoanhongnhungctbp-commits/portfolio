Scope

  • v2: login removed, because it is a personal tool that runs on the device with no server
  • Added morning shift (8 hours), afternoon shift (4 hours), leave day, attendance period by cutoff date, CSV import/export and per-day record editing

Business flow
  1. Daily reminder at the configured time (default 08:45), skipped if already checked in
  2. Choose shift: morning, afternoon or leave
  3. Morning: counts toward 8 net hours; at 12:00 the ring pauses and turns orange, at 13:00 it resumes
  4. Afternoon: before 12:00 check-in is disabled with a countdown; from 12:00 it counts 4 net hours
  5. Leave: records the full day and cancels all reminders for the day
  6. At 18:00, one reminder if checked in but not checked out
  7. Check-out: calculates gross and net hours and updates the day status

BPMN diagram ↗BRD v2 · proposed process ↗Interactive BPMN ↗

Business rules
  • Gross hours = check-out minus check-in, used in report, widget and day detail
  • Net hours = gross minus 1 hour if check-out is after 13:00 on morning shift, used for the progress ring and shift goal
  • Fixed lunch break 12:00–13:00 in phase 1, handled entirely on the device
  • 4 day statuses: on-time (both in and out), incomplete (one missing), missing (none), leave; weekends and holidays are not counted
  • Attendance period by cutoff day 1–28, for example cutoff 26 runs from the 26th of last month to the 25th of this month
  • 4 reminders: start of day, 1 hour left, shift complete, 18:00; checking out under 4 hours on morning shift needs confirmation
  • Profile settings save only when Save is tapped, never automatically
